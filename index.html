RESEARCH PROGRAMME / 05

Research overview.

The questions, evidence and experiments guiding the NEUROCORE investigation.

RESEARCH BASIS · AUG 2026
01
REFINED RESEARCH GAP

How do adaptive SNN policies trade accuracy for computation across real hardware?

The central contribution is a rigorous, reproducible characterization of the accuracy–computation–energy trade-off surface, with computational proxies explicitly separated from true energy measurements.

CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What we want to find out

RQ / 01

Execution strategy

How does event-driven execution compare with dense ANN and time-stepped SNN baselines at matched accuracy and varying input sparsity?

RQ / 02

Adaptive threshold

Can activity-dependent threshold adaptation reduce spike counts while maintaining acceptable accuracy?

RQ / 03

Temporal resolution

Can adaptive temporal binning reduce computation under low activity without losing important signal information?

RQ / 04

Early exit

What accuracy–computation trade-off is achieved when inference stops once confidence is sufficient?

METHODOLOGY

Reproducibility by design

  • Multiple random seeds and repeated runs.
  • Warm-up runs excluded from timing.
  • Hardware and software configuration recorded.
  • Baseline comparisons on the same device.
  • Confidence intervals and statistical tests.
  • Proxy metrics reported separately from true energy.
RESEARCH POSITION

What NEUROCORE does not claim

Event-driven SNN runtimes, adaptive thresholds, dynamic timesteps and early exit already exist in prior work. NEUROCORE does not claim these individual mechanisms as new.

The intended contribution is their integrated, pluggable evaluation and reproducible cross-hardware trade-off characterization.
