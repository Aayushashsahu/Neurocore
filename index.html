RESEARCH PILLARS

Core platform capabilities

Key architectural innovations
⚡

Event-Driven Sparsity

State evaluations occur only upon arrival of incoming synaptic spikes. Zero activity translates directly into zero compute cycles, eliminating redundant floating-point operations in low-salience temporal periods.

🎯

Dynamic Adaptive Policies

Activity-dependent threshold modulation and temporal bin scaling ensure optimal sensitivity during quiet intervals while stabilizing firing rates during burst activity to conserve energy and compute bandwidth.

📊

Rigorous Proxy Profiling

Strict architectural separation between computational proxies (synaptic events, neuron updates) and physical device measurements (wall-clock latency, Joules), avoiding ungrounded energy claims.

Measurement integrity comes first.

Event-driven scheduling incurs queue insertion and buffer management overhead. Fewer modeled state updates do not automatically guarantee lower wall-clock latency or Joules on commodity hardware. True physical energy and latency must be measured with physical instruments on the exact target backend.

RESEARCH PROGRAMME / 05

Research overview.

The questions, evidence and experiments guiding the NEUROCORE investigation.

RESEARCH BASIS · 2026
01
REFINED RESEARCH GAP

How do adaptive SNN policies trade accuracy for computation across real hardware?

The central contribution is a rigorous, reproducible characterization of the accuracy–computation–energy trade-off surface, with computational proxies explicitly separated from physical energy measurements.

CORE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What we want to find out

RQ / 01

Execution strategy

How does event-driven execution compare with dense ANN and time-stepped SNN baselines at matched accuracy and varying input sparsity?

RQ / 02

Adaptive threshold

Can activity-dependent threshold adaptation reduce spike counts while maintaining acceptable inference accuracy?

RQ / 03

Temporal resolution

Can adaptive temporal binning reduce computation under low activity without losing critical signal information?

RQ / 04

Early exit

What accuracy–computation trade-off is achieved when inference terminates early once output confidence is sufficient?

METHODOLOGY

Reproducibility by design

  • Multiple random seeds and repeated runs.
  • Warm-up runs excluded from timing.
  • Hardware and software configuration recorded.
  • Baseline comparisons on the exact same device.
  • Confidence intervals and statistical tests.
  • Proxy metrics reported separately from physical energy.
RESEARCH POSITION

What NEUROCORE does not claim

Event-driven SNN runtimes, adaptive thresholds, dynamic timesteps and early exit already exist in prior literature. NEUROCORE does not claim these individual mechanisms as new.

The intended contribution is their integrated, pluggable evaluation and reproducible cross-hardware trade-off characterization.
