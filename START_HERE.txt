NEUROCORE — QUICK START

1. Extract neurocore-platform.zip.
2. Open the extracted neurocore-platform folder.
3. On Windows, double-click start-neurocore.bat.
4. Your browser should open http://localhost:8000.

Alternative: double-click index.html to open the static demo directly.

For your presentation:
- Start on Overview.
- Open Simulation lab and click Run simulation.
- Change sparsity and execution mode to demonstrate the interaction.
- Open Benchmark lab and adjust the sparsity slider.
- Finish with Architecture and Hardware to explain what is implemented and what is planned.

Remember: the browser simulation and benchmark counters are illustrative prototypes, not measured hardware results.
